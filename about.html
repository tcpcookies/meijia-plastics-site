About Us

A Hong Kong supplier of soft floor goods and foam recovery tools

MEIJIA PLASTICS PRODUCTS HK LIMITED works with retailers, importers and private-label brands who need printed mats, runner rugs and massage rollers produced to a defined specification — not bought off a catalogue shelf.

Who We Are

Hong Kong office, South China production

We are a Hong Kong registered company (美嘉塑膠製品香港有限公司) supplying plastic and foam-based home and fitness goods to overseas buyers. Our sales and export office sits in Hong Kong; manufacturing is arranged with production partners in South China, which keeps tooling, sampling and bulk runs inside one time zone and one working week.

Our focus is deliberately narrow. Rather than offering thousands of SKUs, we build depth in three product families — printed crystal-velvet mats, plush runner rugs and solid EVA rollers — so that a single purchase order can cover a full soft-goods programme.

Buyers normally come to us with a design already in mind: a character programme, a marketplace listing that needs a private label, or a retail range that has to hit a target landed cost. We turn that into an approved sample, then into a shipment.

Capabilities

What we can do in-house and with our production partners

Printing & artwork

Full-surface printing of the crystal-velvet face, including character repeats, edge lettering and directional layouts. We proof artwork before any material is cut.

Foam, lamination & backing

Cushioned foam cores bonded to printed faces, with a moulded point-plastic dot pattern applied to the reverse for grip on hard flooring.

Cutting & shaping

Straight-cut rectangles, scalloped cloud edges and wavy borders for runners; solid EVA barrels turned and profiled for rollers.

Packing & export

Rolled or flat-packed, retail-ready cartons, barcode and FNSKU labelling, and full export documentation for FOB, CIF or DDP terms.

Quality & Compliance

Three checkpoints before anything ships

Compliance is agreed in the quotation, not discovered at the port. We produce to the standard you nominate and coordinate third-party testing against it.

01

Incoming material check

Foam density, fabric hand feel, backing compound and colour are verified against the approved sample before a production run starts.

02

In-line print & backing inspection

Print registration, colour consistency and dot-pattern coverage are checked during the run, not only at the end of it.

03

Pre-shipment final inspection

Dimensions, seams, packing configuration and carton marking are confirmed against your approved specification before the container is loaded.

Standards We Quote Against
REACH (EU / UK) EN71 (toys & children's items) CPSIA (United States) Third-party lab testing Buyer-specified inspection
