What helps us quote accurately the first time

01

Product and size

Which family — mat, runner or roller — plus the dimensions and thickness you need. If you have a reference product, a photo is enough to start.

02

Artwork status

Whether the design is final, still in development, or licensed from a third party. Licensing authorisation is required before production of character artwork.

03

Destination and terms

The market you are selling into (this sets the test standard) and whether you need FOB, CIF or DDP pricing.
