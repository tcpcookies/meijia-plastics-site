Why Buyers Work With Us

Built around the details that fail at retail

Most claims on a soft floor mat come down to three things: whether the print holds, whether the backing grips, and whether the roll ships flat. Here is how we handle each.

01

Artwork printed to the edge

Full-surface printing across the crystal-velvet face, including character repeats, borders and directional layouts. We work from your vector file or licensed asset pack.

02

Point-plastic anti-slip backing

A dense dot pattern moulded onto the reverse holds the mat on tile, laminate and polished concrete without a separate underlay.

03

Quiet, shock-absorbing cushion

The foam core damps impact for yoga, pilates and floor work, and reduces the noise of dropped weights and footsteps in apartments.

04

Solid-core rollers, not hollow tubes

Our massage rollers are solid EVA rather than thin-walled shells, so the node pattern keeps its shape under repeated body weight.

05

Dual-mode node geometry

A central crescent zone follows the curve of the back, while twin shoulder peaks carry 168 raised contact points for broader muscle coverage.

06

Compliance built to your market

We produce against the standard you nominate — REACH, EN71 or CPSIA — and coordinate third-party lab testing before shipment. Test scope is agreed in the quotation.

Buyer Questions

Answers before you ask

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the product, print method and packaging. Printed mats and rollers carry a different minimum from plain-colour goods, and mixed-design orders are quoted as a single run. Tell us the quantity you are planning and we will quote against that number rather than a generic figure.

Can you print licensed characters or brand artwork?

We print to customer-supplied files. Where artwork contains a third-party character, logo or trademark, we ask for written authorisation from the rights holder before production. This protects both your business and ours at customs and at retail.

What sizes and thicknesses can you produce?

Exercise mats, runners and rollers are all made to your dimensions. Standard roller size is 45 × 12.5 cm diameter in solid EVA; mats and runners are quoted per size and thickness from your tech pack.

How does the anti-slip backing work?

The reverse carries a moulded point-plastic dot pattern. The dots compress slightly under body weight and create friction against hard flooring, so the mat stays put on tile and laminate without an extra grip layer.

Do you supply samples?

Yes. Stock samples of existing constructions can be sent for evaluation, and a pre-production sample in your own artwork is made after the order is confirmed. Sample cost and courier terms are stated in the quotation.

How are mats and rollers packed?

Mats are rolled with a paper or film wrap and packed in printed cartons or poly bags per your requirement; rollers are cartoned individually or in pairs. Retail-ready packaging with barcodes, hangtags and Amazon FNSKU labels can be arranged.

Which test reports can you support?

We produce to the standard you nominate — commonly REACH for the EU, EN71 for toys and children's items, and CPSIA for the United States — and coordinate testing through third-party laboratories. The exact test scope is agreed and written into the quotation before production.

What are your payment and shipping terms?

Terms are set per order based on quantity and whether tooling is involved. We ship FOB from South China ports and can quote CIF or DDP on request. Full terms are confirmed in the proforma invoice.
