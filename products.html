03 — Recovery & Massage

Solid EVA Foam Rollers

High-density solid EVA rollers for post-workout myofascial release. The dual-mode node geometry combines a central crescent zone that follows the curve of the spine with twin-peak shoulders for broader muscle coverage.

Standard construction

ColourwaysPink marble, matte white, custom
PackingIndividual carton or pair-packed
Node Geometry

Why the surface pattern matters

A single-pattern roller either digs in too hard or glides without effect. Our tooling splits the barrel into two zones so one roller covers both deep and broad work.

Zonal breakdown
Crescent zone (centre)11 crescent-shaped nodes; arc follows the lumbar and thoracic curve; relieves muscle tension along the spine
Twin-peak zones (flanks)168 three-dimensional contact points; 360° wrap-around pressure; wider coverage for quads, calves and IT band
Node profileRaised pyramids with rounded tips — firm pressure without sharp edges
CoreSolid EVA, holds shape under repeated body weight
